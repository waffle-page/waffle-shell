## 2. What the service is

Waffle is a **local-first library** for your notes, links and files, kept as
ordinary files in an ordinary folder on your own device.

- **The app** — for Mac, iPhone and Android, the browser extension, and the web
  app at app.waffle.page. It works with no account and no server, and is free.
- **An optional account** — sign-in by email. Signing in uploads nothing.
- **Personal Sync** — your library, end-to-end encrypted, on all your devices.
  A paid add-on (section 5). Personal Sync is not switched on yet.
- **Shared folders** — a folder you share with people you invite, end-to-end
  encrypted for its members. Shared folders are not switched on yet.

**No uptime commitment.** There is no service level.

## 3. Your content is yours

- **Your library is a normal folder.** Everything Waffle writes stays readable
  by other programs, and you do not need Waffle to read your own data.
- **Waffle claims no ownership of your content and no licence to it.** Our
  servers hold what you sync or share only to store it and carry it between your
  devices and the people you share with, and they hold it encrypted.
- **Deleting goes to Trash first.** Deleted files move to a Trash folder inside
  your library, where you can restore them. Trash empties itself after 30 days,
  and "Delete forever" removes something sooner. Nothing else in the app deletes
  your files for good.
- **Keep your own backups.** Your library is an ordinary folder, so ordinary
  backup tools work on it. Sync is not a backup: deleting on one device deletes
  on all of them.

- An account is **optional and always will be**. Using Waffle without one is a

- You sign in with a **link we email you**. There is no password.
- **Signing in is not permission to upload.** Sync and sharing are separate
  steps, each with its own consent.
- You are responsible for the email account you sign in with: access to it is
  access to your Waffle account, including the ability to ask for its deletion

- **Your encryption keys are separate from your account.** Getting your email
  back does not by itself recover your encryption keys. If you lose every device
  and your recovery code, your encrypted data cannot be recovered — including by

## 5. Fees

The app is free, on one device or many, with no account.

**Personal Sync is a paid add-on.** Its prices, plans, billing period, renewal,
cancellation and refund terms are not set yet; none is stated here until they
are, and nothing can be bought before then. Payment is taken by Stripe on the
web and in the Mac app, and by Apple in the iPhone and iPad app; a purchase
through Apple or Google is also governed by their terms.

**Storage.** Each library can store up to **5 GB** with Sync. The encrypted
bytes of a replaced or deleted file version are kept **30 days** after it is
replaced or deleted, then removed, and count towards the 5 GB until then; for a
deleted file the 30 days start when the deletion reaches our servers, not when
it leaves your Trash. Files deleted together with their folder are not
removed this way yet: their bytes stay, and count, until you delete your
account.
Small preview images do not count.

**When you reach the limit**, editing on your devices continues, new uploads
pause, everything already stored stays downloadable, and nothing on your
devices is deleted.

If you use your own key with an outside AI service, **you pay that service
directly**, and Waffle is not part of that arrangement.

## 6. Acceptable use

- **Do not use the browser extension or Waffle's saving features to crawl or
  scrape sites in bulk.** Saving pages you are looking at is the intended use.
- **Pages you save are your private copies.** Waffle never publishes them;
  republishing someone else's content is your responsibility.
- **Do not try to get around Waffle's security boundaries** or the access rules
  of shared folders. If you find a flaw, report it to support@waffle.page.
- **Do not use shared folders to distribute unlawful content.** Waffle cannot
  read shared content — it holds it encrypted — so it cannot moderate it, and
  responsibility rests with you.
- Respect other people's rights in what you save and share.

## 9. Ending, and deleting your account

- **You can stop at any time.** Sign out, or simply stop using the app; your
  library is a folder you already control.
- **You can delete your account** in the app, or at waffle.page/delete-account
  with only your email address. **Deleting your account starts a 7-day hold**:
  the account is frozen — Sync stops, and signing in offers only "Cancel
  deletion" — and we email you a link to cancel, a reminder the day before, and
  a confirmation once it is deleted. After the hold, what Waffle stores for the
  account is deleted and cannot be restored.
- **Deleting your account does not delete everything.** What is kept, for how
  long and why — our 30-day backup, the deleted account's random identifier,
  the records other members of shared folders keep as their own, and our
  database provider's own logs and backups — is in the Privacy Policy, section
  "Deletion". A shared folder you own that others still use must be handed over
  or erased first.
- **Files freed from a device.** A file you removed from a device with "Free up
  space" exists only on our servers until a device downloads it again; deleting
  your account deletes it.
- **Ending a paid subscription** does not delete your data, and deleting your
  account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google.
- **Suspension.** No grounds on which Waffle would suspend or end an account
  have been written yet.
