# Waffle Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-10-13. Effective on publication.** The same text ships with the app as PRIVACY.txt (Settings → About), is linked from the sign-in screen beside the email field, and is published at waffle.page/privacy.

---

## The short version

- Your library is a folder of ordinary files on your own device. Waffle works fully without an account, and in that mode nothing about you reaches us.
- If you sign in and turn on **Personal Sync**, the contents and names of your notes and files are encrypted on your device with keys only your devices hold. Our servers and providers cannot read them. What is *not* encrypted — your email address, when you signed in, sizes and times — is listed here, because "encrypted" does not mean "invisible". **Shared folders** are not offered at launch; section 10 describes how they will work.
- There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking in the app or on the website. We set no cookies.
- We never sell or share your personal data for advertising, and we never use your content to train AI.
- Our servers are in the United States. We tell you exactly which companies process data for us and why.
- You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the website. This policy says what is kept afterwards and for how long.
- Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to a regulator.

## 4. Your library lives on your device

- **Your files are the real thing.** Notes are Markdown files; everything else is an ordinary file in an ordinary folder. You can open the folder with any other program without Waffle running.
- **Waffle's index is disposable.** To be fast, Waffle keeps an index of your library in the app's own storage, never inside your library. It can be rebuilt from your files at any time.
- **Deleting goes to Trash first.** Deleting moves a file to a Trash folder inside your library, where you can restore it. Trash empties itself after 30 days, and "Delete forever" removes something sooner. Those are the only ways Waffle deletes a file for good. "Free up space" removes only this device's downloaded copy of a file Sync can give back.
- **Waffle does not measure or meter your local library.**
- **In a web browser, storage is weaker.** The web app keeps your library inside the browser, where you cannot see it as files, cannot back it up, and the browser may clear it. The web app is meant for trying Waffle and saving things quickly; the Mac app keeps your library in a real folder.

## 9. Personal Sync

Personal Sync keeps your library on all your devices. Your files stay on your devices; what travels between them, through our servers, is encrypted first.

**What our servers can see:** your account and the numbers that identify your libraries, devices and their approvals; your devices' public keys (the halves meant to be shared); the size of each change and when it arrived; which item each change belongs to, and whether it was a deletion; how much storage you use; and the encrypted changes themselves.

**What our servers never receive:** the contents or names of your notes and files, your encryption keys, or your recovery code.

**The limits of encryption.** Encryption keeps your content unreadable to our storage providers acting alone. It cannot guarantee that a server always shows your devices every latest change: a misbehaving server could hold back or repeat changes, though it still could not read them. And the pattern of when, and how much, you sync is visible.

**Where it is stored.** The database and sign-in are run by Supabase in the United States. The encrypted bytes of your files are stored in Cloudflare R2, in R2's United States jurisdiction.

**History.** Sync keeps the encrypted history of every note and file — each earlier version, and the record that something was deleted — for as long as your account exists, so that you can go back in time. One exception: the encrypted bytes of a file version are removed from storage 30 days after the version is replaced, or 30 days after the file is deleted. For a deleted file the 30 days start when the deletion reaches our servers, not when the file leaves your Trash: emptying Trash or "Delete forever" does not remove them sooner, and restoring a file from Trash does not depend on them, because your device uploads it again. When two devices change the same file at the same time, the 30 days for the version that loses start when one of your devices next syncs. A replaced or deleted version is removed only once every device on your account has synced since it was replaced or deleted (a device whose storage is full still counts as syncing; a device holding changes from your other devices that it has not applied yet does not, and says so in its Activity), so a device that has stopped syncing keeps them stored, and counting towards your storage, until it syncs again or you remove it from your account. A file deleted together with its folder is deleted for good 30 days after the folder, by the device that deleted the folder, the next time that device syncs. Those 30 days are counted by that device's clock, from when it deleted the folder or first saw that it had, whichever is later, so they are 30 real days on a correct clock. The bytes' own 30 days start then, so they go about 60 days after the folder. If that device never syncs again (it is lost or wiped, or Sync is turned off on it), they stay, and count towards your storage, until you delete your account; so, more rarely, does a file deleted while another device was changing it. While a replaced or deleted version is kept, its bytes count towards your storage only where kept versions come to more than your plan's storage, so deleting a file frees its space at once.

**Turning Sync off on a device** stops that device syncing. It does not delete what our servers hold for your account; deleting your account does.

**If you lose everything, it is gone.** A new device is approved by one of your devices or by your recovery code. If you lose every device and the recovery code, your encrypted data cannot be recovered — by you or by us.

## 10. Shared folders

A shared folder works differently from your own library: our servers hold the folder, encrypted for its members, and each member's devices keep their own copy.

**Shared folders reveal more than Sync does.** Our servers can see:

- that an invitation exists, who sent it, and when it expires;
- who tried to accept which invitation — including invitations that were never approved. This cannot be hidden, because recording who accepted is what stops an invitation being reused;
- which folder each encrypted item belongs to, who added it and when, and its size;
- who is a member of which folder, with what role, since when, and when that ended;
- when members' keys change;
- who handed a folder over to whom, and when.

**Other members are other people.** What you add to a shared folder is readable on every member's devices. Removing someone stops them seeing new changes; it cannot take back what their devices already hold.

**Storage for a shared folder counts against its owner**, not against every member.

## 11. Payments and plans

Personal Sync comes with every account: Free includes 1 GB; Plus ($4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, 10 GB) and Pro ($9.99 a month or $95.99 a year, 100 GB) add storage. Nothing can be bought yet.

When you pay, the payment is handled by whoever you pay through: **Stripe** on the web and from the Mac app, **Apple** in the iPhone and iPad app, and **Google** in the Android app. Your card or account details go to them, never to Waffle.

- **Stripe** acts as the merchant of record for web purchases and sees your email address, the plan and price, your payment method, your billing country or address (for tax), and your IP address and device details on its checkout pages. It sends receipts from Link, keeps its own transaction records for as long as the law requires, and can delete your payment data on request. Stripe's privacy policy and Link's terms apply to those pages.
- **Apple and Google** receive a random token that links the purchase to your Waffle account; they do not receive your email address from us. They keep their own records under their policies.
- **What Waffle learns from them:** whether your subscription is active, until when, through which seller, and the reference number of each billing event (purchase, renewal, failed payment, cancellation, refund, dispute). Waffle never holds amounts or card details.

**Emails about your plan.** We email you a confirmation when you subscribe, a reminder at least 30 days before every yearly renewal, and notices when a payment fails, when a plan ends and — if you are storing more than the free 1 GB after a plan ends — at about 30, 60 and 83 days before the online copy of your library is removed on day 90 (a file kept only online, because every device freed it with "Free up space", goes with it). A Free account with no sign-in for 12 months is deleted after an email at 11 months and another two weeks before. These are service emails about your account, not marketing; you cannot opt out of them while you have the account.

A subscription bought through Apple or Google is managed and cancelled there; deleting your Waffle account does not cancel it.

## 12. AI features

AI in Waffle runs on your device. Using your own key with an outside AI service is optional and never happens by itself.

- **Search, image recognition and transcription run on your device**, using models downloaded only after you agree.
- **Transcripts and other AI results stay on your device.** They are not shared or synced.
- **In a web browser, outside AI services cannot be used at all**: the web app has nowhere to keep a key.
- **If you use your own key in the Mac or phone app**, each request first shows the provider, the model, what will be sent, how long the provider keeps it (or that it has not said), and the estimated time and cost. Only what you selected is sent, directly from your device to that provider, under that provider's terms and privacy policy; Waffle's servers are not involved. Health, financial, contact and password-type data stays on your device by default.
- **Waffle never trains AI on your content**, and no AI provider receives anything from Waffle's servers.
- **No account is needed for any of this.**

## 13. Saving things from the web

**The app itself does not look pages up.** Saving a link saves the link; Waffle does not fetch the page, its title or its icon, because that would tell someone what you save.

**The browser extension** works only on the tab you click it on. It reads that page's address, title and the summary the page publishes about itself — not the text of the page. It cannot see your other tabs, your history or your cookies.

**Two requests happen after you save, and they are named here.**

- **The extension fetches the page's preview picture**, without cookies and without telling that server which page you were on.
- **On iPhone and iPad, the app may fetch one preview picture** a few minutes after you share a page to Waffle, if the page named one. That server sees your IP address and the time, and nothing that identifies you or Waffle: no cookies, no account, no device or language details.

## 18. Deletion

**On your device.** Deleting a note or file moves it to Trash inside your own library, where you can restore it. Trash empties itself after 30 days, and "Delete forever" removes something sooner. On a Mac, removing Waffle does not delete your library, and you do not need Waffle to read it. On a phone, and in a browser, the library lives in Waffle's own storage, so removing Waffle or clearing the browser's data deletes it; the [Cookie and Storage Notice](https://waffle.page/cookies/) says how to keep a copy.

**Your account.** You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account), or at waffle.page/delete-account with only your email address.

- **A 7-day hold comes first.** Deleting your account starts a 7-day hold. During it your account is frozen: Sync stops on every device, and signing in offers only "Cancel deletion". We email you when the hold starts, with a link to cancel, and again the day before deletion. Cancelling restores the account as it was.
- **Then everything Waffle stores for the account is deleted, keys first.** The keys we hold for your account are destroyed before anything else, so what remains cannot be opened by a new device. Then your synced notes and files and their history, your devices and sign-ins, every shared folder you own that nobody else uses, and the account itself are deleted.
- **What is kept, and for how long** — the same list the deletion page and the app's deletion receipt give:
  - **30 days:** our nightly backup still holds your account's records until it expires.
  - **Up to 33 days:** our second copy of stored files, at a separate backup storage provider, still holds your account's encrypted files for at most 33 days after they left our storage.
  - **Up to 47 days:** that backup provider keeps a one-way fingerprint of your account's internal sign-in identifier and the day it was deleted, so that restoring an older backup cannot bring your account back. It contains nothing about you, and is deleted within 47 days.
  - **Indefinitely:** a random number that identified your account, and when it was deleted, so no new account can take its place. It contains nothing about you.
  - **For the life of the folder:** in shared folders other people own, the record that you were a member — your invitations, your access (ended, not erased) and what you added there are the other members' to keep.
  - **For the life of those folders:** the public keys other members use to check what you shared with them. They open nothing.
  - **Until its current owner erases it:** a folder you started and handed to someone else still names you as the one who started it. A folder you owned alone is deleted, leaving only a short fingerprint so it cannot be recreated.
  - **Indefinitely:** a shared folder whose hand-over was left unfinished, which nobody can now erase. The receipt names any such folder.
  - **For our database provider's own period:** its sign-in audit log, which records each sign-in with its IP address and the account it belongs to. Waffle cannot delete it or see how long it is kept; during the beta the provider keeps its logs about a day, and from public launch about a week.
  - **For our database provider's own period:** its own backups — none during the beta, and from public launch its daily backups, kept about a week.
- **A shared folder you own that other people still use** has to be handed over or erased first, from a device where you are signed in; deletion stops and names it until you do.
- **What stays with you.** Your files on your own devices — Waffle never deletes them for you. One exception: a file you removed from a device with "Free up space" exists only on our servers until a device downloads it again, so deleting your account deletes it. Open such files on a device first if you want to keep them. A device where you are still signed in keeps its own copy of your keys until you remove Waffle's data there, and a recovery code you wrote down is still on paper.

**Payment records.** Deleting your account cancels a web subscription at the end of its period and asks Stripe to delete your payment data; a store subscription must be cancelled in the store. Stripe, Apple and Google keep the records the law requires them to keep.

## 19. Backups

Every night the database is copied — every record, including account email addresses and the encrypted Sync and shared-folder records — and the copy is kept privately at GitHub for 30 days, then deleted. The copy is encrypted with a key Waffle holds, and leaves out sign-in sessions, sign-in links and codes. Your content inside it is encrypted either way. A deleted account stays in the backups until they expire. The encrypted bytes of stored files are copied each night to a separate backup storage provider in the United States and kept there for at most 33 days after they leave our storage. If we ever restore a backup, accounts deleted since it was made are deleted again before anyone can use it: for that, the provider keeps a one-way fingerprint of each deleted account's sign-in identifier for at most 47 days.

## 20. Security

Your content is encrypted end to end with modern, published algorithms, with keys that never leave your devices unencrypted. Everything between your device and our servers travels over TLS. Access to our providers' consoles is limited to Waffle's operator, protected by multi-factor sign-in, and never used to read user records except to answer a request of yours or to investigate abuse. We keep no copy of your keys and no password of yours. No system is perfectly secure: **if we learn of a breach that affects your personal data, we will tell you without undue delay, and tell the regulators the law requires within the time it requires** (72 hours under the GDPR). Report security problems to contact@waffle.page.

## 21. Your rights

These rights are yours wherever you live. Where a law such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or a U.S. state privacy law (California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others) gives you more, you have that too.

**You can:**

- **know** what personal data we hold about you and get a copy (most of it is already on your own device; the rest is what sections 7–11 list);
- **correct** it (write to us to change your email address);
- **delete** it — your account, in the app or at waffle.page/delete-account, without asking us; or by emailing us;
- **take it with you**: your library is already an ordinary folder on your device; we can also give you the account records we hold in a machine-readable form;
- **restrict or object** to processing based on our legitimate interests, and we will stop unless we have a compelling reason that overrides your interests;
- **withdraw consent** at any time where we rely on it (the per-request AI consent), with no effect on what was done before;
- **opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data and of targeted advertising** — we do not do any of these, so there is nothing to opt out of; we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as such an opt-out anyway;
- **limit the use of sensitive personal data** — we do not use any;
- **not be discriminated against** for exercising any right.

**How to exercise them.** Email contact@waffle.page from the address on your account, or state the address and we will send a code to it. That is how we verify it is you; we ask for nothing more unless the request is unusual. An authorised agent may act for you if you confirm the authorisation to us from your account's address. We answer within one month (or 45 days where U.S. state law sets that period), and tell you if we need longer. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive.
